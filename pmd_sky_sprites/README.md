# PMD Sky Sprites

PMDCollab SpriteCollab's community-drawn animation sheets dress the
sky family: wild flyers (Wild Skies) and your FLY mount (Free Fly)
wear species-true, 8-direction PMD art in the air.

**Community art only.** Sheets whose current SpriteCollab credit is
Spike Chunsoft (the official sprites imported from the PMD games) are
excluded at bake time, so this mod ships nothing by Nintendo or its
partners. The honest consequence: coverage today is thin (10 species
of the first 251), because the collab's gen 1-2 locomotion sheets are
mostly still official art awaiting community redraws. The pack grows
automatically as redraws land: re-run `scripts/pmd_pack.py` in the
monorepo and repack.

Sheets are licensed CC BY-NC 4.0 by their contributors; see
CREDITS.md and LICENSE-SPRITES.md.

On a mount whose sheet carries all eight facing rows, Free Fly 1.7.0+
unlocks diagonal flight: hold two directions and the mount faces and
flies the true diagonal.
