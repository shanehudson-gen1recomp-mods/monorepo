# PMD Sky Sprites

PMDCollab SpriteCollab's community-drawn animation sheets dress the
sky family: wild flyers (Wild Skies) and your FLY mount (Free Fly)
wear species-true, 8-direction PMD art in the air.

**Community art only ships.** Sheets whose current SpriteCollab credit
is Spike Chunsoft (the official sprites imported from the PMD games)
are excluded at bake time, so this mod ships nothing by Nintendo or
its partners. The honest consequence: packed coverage is thin, because
the collab's gen 1-2 locomotion sheets are mostly still official art
awaiting community redraws. The pack grows automatically as redraws
land: re-run `scripts/pmd_pack.py` in the monorepo and repack.

Sheets are licensed CC BY-NC 4.0 by their contributors; see
CREDITS.md and LICENSE-SPRITES.md.

**Your own cartridge fills the gaps.** Drop a dump of your Explorers
of Sky (US) cartridge (a `.nds` file) into this mod's folder and the
mod reads the official in-air sheets straight out of it while you
play: species the community pack doesn't cover yet fly in real PMD
art, from your cart. The sheets exist only in memory; nothing is
extracted to disk and nothing official ever ships with the mod.
Community sheets still win where they exist, and the IMPORT PMD
toggle in the mod's settings turns the cartridge reading off entirely.

On a mount whose sheet carries all eight facing rows, Free Fly 1.7.0+
unlocks diagonal flight: hold two directions and the mount faces and
flies the true diagonal.
